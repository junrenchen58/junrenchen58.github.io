Hi there - welcome to my homepage! I am currently a Founder’s Postdoc at Department of Statistics, Columbia University. I am broadly interested in high-dimensional statistics, nonconvex optimization, mathematics of data, signal processing, machine learning, randomized algorithms, and related areas.

Previously, I was a Novikov Postdoc in Mathematics at University of Maryland, College Park. Even before that, I received my PhD from the Mathematics Department at the University of Hong Kong, and got my BSc in Mathematics from Sun Yat-sen University.
